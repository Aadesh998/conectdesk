# Getting started

How to sign in, find your way around and use the setup checklist.

## Signing in

Your workspace has its own address, for example `https://brightclasses.desk.arrlink.com`. Open it, enter the email and password you were given, and click **Sign in**.

![The workspace login screen](/guide/img/login.png)
*The login page shows your workspace's name at the top.*

- **Forgot password?** emails you a link to set a new one.
- If your workspace uses **single sign-on**, you'll also see a button such as "Login with Google".
- If **two-factor login** is on, you'll be asked for the 6-digit code from your authenticator app after your password.

> Don't know your workspace address? Go to `https://desk.arrlink.com`, type your workspace name and you'll be sent to the right login page.

## Finding your way around

![The inbox screen after signing in](/guide/img/agent-inbox-list.jpg)
*The main screen: the icon bar on the far left, the inbox menu, the conversation list and the open conversation.*

The thin bar on the far left switches between the main areas:

| Icon | Opens | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Inbox tray | **Inbox** | Answering conversations (everyone uses this). |
| Contact card | **Contacts** | The list of customers and their details. |
| Chart | **Reports** | Response times, SLA, CSAT, call numbers. |
| Shield | **Admin** | Every setting in this guide. Only shown if your role allows at least one setting. |
| Bell (bottom) | **Notifications** | Mentions, assignments and SLA alerts. |
| Your initials (bottom) | **Your account** | Profile, password, two-factor login, availability (Online / Away), log out. |

Inside **Admin**, settings are grouped in a menu. Click a group to open it:

| Group | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | General, Domains, Usage, Admissions, Privacy, Data export, Business hours, SLA policies |
| **Help center** | Your public help-center websites and their articles |
| **AI** | Providers, Snippets, Suggestions, Tools, Assistants |
| **Channel** | Inboxes (chat widget, WhatsApp, email…) |
| **Conversations** | Statuses, Tags, Custom attributes, Shared views |
| **Productivity** | Macros, Automations |
| **Teammates** | Agents, Teams, Roles, Activity logs |
| **Notifications** | Email server for team notifications, email Templates |
| **Security** | SSO (single sign-on) |
| **Integrations** | Webhooks, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Context links |

On the right of most admin pages there's a grey **help text** that sums up what the page is for.

## The setup checklist

New workspaces show a **Set up your workspace** card at the bottom of the inbox menu ("2 of 5 done"). Click it, or go to *Admin → Workspace → General* and open `/admin/setup`.

![The setup checklist](/guide/img/adm-setup.jpg)
*Five first steps. Each **Start** button takes you straight to the right screen, and finished steps are ticked off automatically.*

| Step | What it asks you to do |
|---|---|
| **Connect an inbox** | Bring in conversations from email, live chat, WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram. |
| **Invite a teammate** | Add the agents who'll answer customers with you. |
| **Install the chat widget** | Add a live chat inbox and put its code on your website. Done when the first chat arrives. |
| **Publish a help center article** | Write and publish one article. |
| **Reply to a customer** | Send your first reply. |

When everything is done, or if you don't need the card, click **Hide checklist**.

## Your own account

Click your initials at the bottom-left, then **Account**.

![The profile page](/guide/img/agent-account.jpg)
*Change your name, phone and photo here.*

- **Profile:** your first and last name and your **Public avatar** (what customers see when you reply on chat), and **Phone for calls** (with country code, e.g. +91…) if your workspace uses phone calls. To change your email or roles, ask an admin.
- **Security:** turn on **two-factor login** with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator. Save the **recovery codes** it shows you somewhere safe — they're the only way in if you lose your phone. (An admin can reset it for you under *Admin → Teammates → Agents* if you lose both.)

The same menu also has:

- **Away** switch: while it's on, team round robin skips you, so you can take a break. (After 5 minutes without activity your status also shows Away, and returns to Online when you're back.)
- **Reassign replies** switch: for leave or long absences — customer replies on your conversations go to someone else.
- **Dark Mode**, **Keyboard shortcuts** and **Logout**.
