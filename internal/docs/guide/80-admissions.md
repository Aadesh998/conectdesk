# Admissions

Tools for coaching institutes, schools and anyone who turns enquiries into sign-ups: lead stages, a daily WhatsApp report, AI for calls, and a one-click AI admissions setup.

**Where:** *Admin → Workspace → Admissions*.

![The Admissions settings page](/guide/img/adm-admissions.jpg)

## Lead stages

The steps an enquiry goes through, in order. They're the columns on the **Leads** board your counsellors use.

- Each stage has a **name** and a **kind**: **In progress**, **Admitted** (counts as an admission in reports) or **Lost** (won't join).
- Use ▲ / ▼ to reorder, **Add stage** to add one (up to 12), × to remove one (only once it has no leads).
- **Put new enquiries on the first stage**: everyone who calls or writes for the first time lands on the first stage automatically.
- You need at least one In progress and one Admitted stage. Click **Save**.

A typical set: `New` → `Interested` → `Visit booked` → `Admitted` / `Lost`.

## Daily report on WhatsApp

Sends the owner the day's numbers every evening: new enquiries, calls, missed calls, call-backs still waiting and admissions.

1. Turn on **Send the daily report** and choose **Send at** (in your workspace time zone).
2. Pick the **WhatsApp inbox** to send from and an approved **Template** — or click **Create the suggested template** and wait for Meta to approve it.
3. **Send to**: up to 10 WhatsApp numbers, separated by commas.
4. **Save**, then **Send today's report now** to test.

## AI for calls and leads

Each uses your AI provider and counts towards your AI usage. They save as soon as you switch them.

- **Summarise recorded calls**: every recorded call is transcribed (Hindi, English or both) and summarised on its call card — what the caller wanted, what was said and the next step.
- **Fill in lead details**: fills a contact's empty fields (course, class, city…) from their chats and calls. Fields someone already filled are never changed.

## One-click AI admissions setup

**Set up the AI admissions assistant** adds what an institute needs to answer enquiries with AI. Only what's missing is added, so it's safe to run again.

| Tick | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Contact fields for admissions** | Student's name, class, course, preferred batch and city. |
| **Knowledge base drafts** | Snippets for courses, fees, timings, admission process, scholarships and results. **They stay off until you fill them in and switch them on.** |
| **An AI assistant** | Answers in Hindi, English or Hinglish, collects the student's details and hands over to a counsellor. Set the **Assistant name**, **Hand over to team**, and which **WhatsApp inboxes** send new chats to it. |

Click **Set up**. Then:

1. Click **Fill them in** to open the draft snippets. Replace every placeholder with your real courses, fees and timings, and turn each one **Enabled**. See [how to write snippets](/guide/knowledge-base#how-to-write-snippets-the-ai-answers-well-from).
2. Click **Open it** to review the assistant, then use its **Test** tab. See [Create your AI assistant](/guide/ai-assistant#step-4-test-before-you-go-live).
