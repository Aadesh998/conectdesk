# Daily work for agents

Share this page with your agents: it covers answering conversations, working with the AI assistant, using Juno, contacts and reports.

## The inbox

![The inbox](/guide/img/agent-inbox-list.jpg)

The menu on the left lists your conversation lists:

| List | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **My Inbox** | Conversations assigned to you. Start your day here. |
| **Mentions** | Conversations where a teammate @mentioned you in a private note. |
| **Unassigned** | Conversations nobody has picked up yet. |
| **All** | Everything you're allowed to see. |
| Team inboxes | Conversations assigned to your team but not yet to a person. |
| **Views** | Saved filters (yours, or shared by an admin). |
| **Call-backs**, **Leads** | Missed calls to return, and the admissions board. |

At the top of the list, pick the status (**Open**, **Snoozed**, **Resolved**…) and the sort order (newest, waiting longest, next SLA deadline…). Hover an avatar to select several conversations and assign, tag or resolve them together.

## Answering a conversation

![A conversation](/guide/img/agent-conversation.jpg)

- The middle shows the thread. The reply box at the bottom has two tabs: **Reply** (goes to the customer) and **Private note** (only your team sees it; type `@` to mention someone).
- Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> to send, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> for the command bar (macros, snooze, assign, resolve…).
- The ✨ (**Generate reply**) button drafts a reply with AI from the conversation — anything you've typed is used as an instruction. Select text and use **AI ▾** to rewrite it (e.g. more polite, shorter).
- The **▾** next to **Send** lets you **Send and set as** Resolved in one step.
- At the top: **Resolve** when you're done, or the status menu to snooze. The ⋮ menu has more actions, including **Summarize with AI**.
- The right sidebar (**Details**) shows the contact, assignee, team, priority and tags, contact fields, previous conversations and notes. **Suggest tags** lets the AI tag it for you.

> **WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram:** you can reply normally within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, the reply box asks you to send an approved template.

## When the AI assistant is handling a chat

![A conversation handled by the AI assistant](/guide/img/agent-ai-conversation.jpg)

A banner above the reply box says **"… (AI) is handling this conversation · replying takes it over"**. You can read along without doing anything. To step in, click **Take over** or just send a reply — the assistant stops replying.

When the assistant hands a chat over to your team, it leaves a private note explaining why. Read it before replying, so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.

## Juno, your AI copilot

Open the **Juno** tab in the right sidebar of any conversation and ask anything — or use a preset such as *Summarize this conversation* or *What is the customer asking?*. Juno can search your knowledge base.

![Juno summarising a conversation](/guide/img/agent-juno-answer.jpg)
*Under each answer: copy, insert into your reply, or add as a private note.*

## Contacts

**Contacts** (the contact-card icon) lists every customer. Open one to see their details, conversations, notes and lead stage, edit their fields, or export / delete their data on request.

![The contacts list](/guide/img/agent-contacts.jpg)

## Reports

**Reports** (the chart icon) shows conversation volume, first-response and resolution times, SLA performance, CSAT and calls, for the period you choose. The **Ask Juno** button answers questions like "Which team breaches the most?".

![Reports overview](/guide/img/agent-reports.jpg)
