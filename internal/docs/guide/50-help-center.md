# Help center

A **help center** is a public website of articles your customers can browse and search — fees, admission steps, how to pay online. Published articles can also feed your [AI assistant](/guide/knowledge-base#help-center-articles), so you write each answer once.

![A public help center with the chat widget open](/guide/img/hc-public.jpg)
*What customers see at `https://<your-workspace>/hc/<slug>`, or on your own domain.*

## Create a help center

**Where:** *Admin → Help center → Help center*.

![The help center list](/guide/img/adm-help-center.jpg)

Click **New** and fill in:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Name** | `Bright Classes Help` |
| **Slug** | The web address part, e.g. `help` → `…/hc/help`. Filled from the name. |
| **Page title** | The browser-tab title, e.g. `Bright Classes – Help Center`. |
| **Template** | **Classic** (big search bar and collection cards — best for customers) or **Docs** (sidebar navigation — best for long manuals). |

Click **Create**. The **Customize** screen opens.

## Customize the look

![The customize screen](/guide/img/hc-customize.jpg)
*Changes show in the live preview on the right. Switch between Landing page / Article page and Desktop / Mobile.*

- **General**: name, slug, **Custom domain** (e.g. `https://help.brightclasses.in`, after verifying it under [Domains](/guide/workspace-settings#domains)), **Meta description** for Google, top **Navigation links**, and **Supported languages**.
- **Appearance**: **Brand** (logo, primary colour, favicon), **Header & hero** (header text, tagline, background), an **Announcement** bar, landing-page layout, article-page options (table of contents, related articles), **Footer** links and social links, and **Custom code** (CSS/JS, e.g. analytics).

Click **Save**.

## Write articles

Click the help center's name to open its **articles tree**.

![The articles tree](/guide/img/hc-tree.jpg)
*Collections are folders; articles sit inside them. Drag the handle to reorder.*

1. **New collection** — e.g. "Admissions", "Fees & payments". Give it a description and an icon, and make sure **Published** is on.
2. In the collection's ⋮ menu, choose **New article**.
3. Write the **Title** and the body (headings, lists, images, tables are all supported).
4. In the right panel:

![The article editor](/guide/img/hc-article-editor.jpg)

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Draft** (only your team sees it) or **Published** (live). |
| **Collection** | Which folder it's in. |
| **Written by** | The author shown to readers — an agent or an AI assistant. |
| **Allow AI assistants to use this article** | Lets the AI answer from this article. Only works while published. |
| **Language** | The article's language. |
| **Excerpt** | A one-line summary under the title and in search results. |
| **SEO & metadata** | Title, description and image for Google and social sharing. |

Click **Create** / **Update**.

**Search insights** (top bar of the tree) shows what customers searched for and **searches with no results** — a ready-made list of articles (and snippets) to write next.

Use **Pause site** in the ⋮ menu to take the whole help center offline without deleting it.
