The cost of a missed call

Most enquiries arrive after the front desk goes home. Every one you miss is ₹20,000 to ₹2,00,000 in fees walking to the institute down the road.

Typical course fees, for one admission

  1. The phone rings at 9 PM

    Nobody picks up, and by the time someone calls back the next afternoon, the parent has already visited somewhere else.

  2. Three apps, no single record

    Calls on one phone, WhatsApp on another, Instagram on someone's laptop. Nobody sees the whole conversation.

  3. No one knows who followed up

    The owner can't tell which leads were called back, which went cold, or how many became admissions.
