Questions institutes ask

We already use a WhatsApp bot. Why this?

Keep it for the easy questions. {{ .Data.Product }} handles what a bot can't: parents who call, missed calls, following up, and showing you who followed up and who didn't. When you're ready, move WhatsApp over too and have everything in one place.

Do we need a new phone number?

Calls come in on an Exotel number, which can be a new one or the one your institute already advertises. Counsellors take calls on their own mobiles.

How long does setup take?

A day. We connect your WhatsApp and phone line, add your counsellors, and set up the AI assistant with your courses and fees.

Is our data safe?

Each institute has its own workspace, with two-factor login, audit logs and a full export of its data any time.
