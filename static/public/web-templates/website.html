This policy explains what personal data {{ .Data.Company }} ("we", "us") collects through the {{ .Data.Product }} website and service, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who this covers

  • Website visitors: anyone browsing {{ .Data.BaseDomain }}{{ with .Data.GuideURL }} or the client guide{{ end }}.
  • Our customers: the institutes and businesses that use {{ .Data.Product }}, and the people they add as users of their workspace.
  • Our customers' contacts: the students, parents and others who call, message or email an institute that uses {{ .Data.Product }}. For their data, the institute decides what is collected and why, and we process it on the institute's behalf and on its instructions. Requests about that data are best sent to the institute; we will help it answer them.

What we collect

  • When you visit the website: the pages you view, the links you click, your browser and device, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We collect this through Google Analytics using cookies, only to understand how the website is used.
  • When you look up a workspace: the workspace name you type, to send you to its login page.
  • When you book a demo: the details you give us, such as your name, institute, phone number and email.
  • When you use a workspace: your name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash), role, sign-in activity and the actions you take, which are kept in the workspace's activity log.
  • What institutes put into {{ .Data.Product }}: conversations from the channels they connect (calls, call recordings and transcripts, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, Telegram, email and website chat), contact details, notes, lead stages and the documents they add to the knowledge base.

How we use it

  • To provide, secure and support the service, including running the AI features an institute turns on.
  • To send service messages, such as password resets and daily reports.
  • To improve the website and the product, using aggregated analytics.
  • To answer your questions and demo requests.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use our customers' conversations to advertise to anyone.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who help us run {{ .Data.Product }}, under contracts that limit what they may do with it:

  • Cloud hosting, database and file storage providers.
  • The messaging and calling providers an institute connects, such as Meta (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger), Telegram, telephony providers and email providers.
  • The AI provider an institute chooses for its AI features.
  • Google Analytics, for website usage statistics.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Cookies

The website uses Google Analytics cookies to count visits and see which pages are useful. We do not use advertising cookies. Workspaces use cookies that are needed to keep you signed in and to protect against forged requests. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work.

How long we keep it

Workspace data is kept for as long as the institute's account is active, and for the retention period the institute sets. When a workspace is deleted, its data is deleted after a short grace period that lets the institute restore it. Website analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by role, sign-ins can require two-factor authentication, and actions are recorded in an activity log. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell affected customers promptly about any breach that concerns them.

Your rights

You can ask us to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct or complete it, to erase it, or to withdraw a consent you gave. You can also nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. To make a request, or to raise a grievance, write to {{ template "website-contact" . }}. We will respond within the time the law requires. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

{{ .Data.Product }} is a tool for institutes, not for children. Where an institute handles the data of students under 18, the institute is responsible for getting the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change, and show the date of the latest change at the top.
